EV Charging Network Planner
Project Report
1. Introduction

The rapid adoption of Electric Vehicles (EVs) has significantly increased the demand for charging infrastructure. However, the growth of charging stations has not kept pace with EV adoption, leading to congestion, uneven coverage, and inefficient infrastructure utilization. This project develops an EV Charging Network Planning System that supports data-driven infrastructure planning by identifying underserved regions, forecasting future charging demand, prioritizing expansion, and recommending strategic investments. The solution combines data analytics, machine learning, geographic analysis, SQL, and Power BI dashboards to assist infrastructure leadership in making informed expansion decisions.

2. Business Problem

The EV infrastructure provider faces several operational challenges, including charging stations operating at very high capacity, long charging queues, unequal distribution of charging stations, limited expansion budgets, and increasing EV adoption. Leadership requires a planning system capable of identifying where new charging stations should be installed, which existing stations require upgrades, and how infrastructure investments can generate maximum business impact.

3. Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are to:

Analyze the current EV charging infrastructure.
Identify underserved regions and coverage gaps.
Discover high-growth EV demand zones.
Predict expansion priorities using machine learning.
Recommend locations for new charging stations.
Build interactive Power BI dashboards.
Generate geographic maps for strategic planning.

11. Strategic Findings

The analysis identified several underserved regions with low charging coverage and increasing EV demand. High-growth areas showed increasing charging sessions and higher utilization, indicating future demand over the next three to five years. Stations with high utilization, long queue lengths, and elevated congestion risk were identified as priority candidates for expansion. Data-driven investment decisions focusing on high-demand and low-coverage locations can significantly improve charging accessibility, reduce waiting times, and increase return on infrastructure investment.

12. Business Recommendations
Install new charging stations in underserved regions.
Upgrade stations experiencing high utilization and long queues.
Prioritize investment in locations with high Expansion Priority Index.
Increase deployment of fast chargers in congested areas.
Continuously monitor station utilization and EV adoption using real-time dashboards.

The EV Charging Network Planner successfully integrates data analytics, SQL, machine learning, geographic analysis, and business intelligence to support strategic infrastructure planning.
